# Round 28e independent five-view review

Verdict: retain the renderer output repair and local warm lamp treatment; return the beam appearance for rework. This is not acceptance of reference 1342 or the whole world.

Independently opened original `ref/1342.png` and all five original 28e GPU images: night reference, beam side, lamp close, reverse and day reference. Adjacent JSON records their hashes, camera metadata, actual beam counts and world identity. No independent rendering was started.

The ALBEDO correction makes real spatial light volumes visible in the reference and side cameras. The close lamp view has a stronger warm optical center and glass haze, while its opaque roof/frame stays in front of distant beam haze. This is limited positive evidence for camera-depth clipping, not comprehensive acceptance of all occlusion. Day reference has no added beam field and its sidecar reports zero beams. Reverse view retains the actual rear island and lighthouse; light direction points largely beyond/away from that camera, so the absence of a conspicuous wedge there is not itself a failure.

The night reference and side view show broad orange bands and pools toward the far ends, with weak near-source continuity. This differs from the restrained pale warm wedge growing from the bright foreground lantern in 1342. The existing density expression explains the tendency: before masks and fade, a perpendicular chord scales approximately with radius times exp(-distance*.0025). Radius grows from .8 m to18.8 m at200 m, making this approximate chord contribution14.3 times the source value. This is an analytic tendency, not a measurement of every actual GPU ray. The local fix should change distance/cross-section behavior and stabilize near-tip sampling, then obtain fresh reference/side/close evidence.

Remaining substantial full-scene gaps are unchanged: clouds are broad rounded slabs; water is dense thin horizontal/triangular sparkle rather than the wider broken bright facets; lighthouse islands are regular tiered plates; coast villages and working shore activity are too sparse. No characters or HUD work is requested. Actual warm water reflections, rotating-beam/time integration, continuous-flight testing and all other reference scenes remain outside this bounded verification.

28f proposed finite-frustum interval formula was separately checked against a double-precision convex-solid oracle for12,030 CPU rays. Root applied the suggested AABB-entry rebase before first GPU use; independently checking the rebased equations reduced maximum tested length error to .02544 m with zero >5 cm errors and zero >1 mm hit-classification mismatches. The exact counterexamples and separate baseline/rebased shader hashes are in `round-28f-independent-interval-check.json`. This is CPU arithmetic evidence rather than a bit-identical GPU emulator or proof of visible appearance. Actual28f renders remain required.
