The maximum angle is **89.58°**, projected area **3.78e-9 m²**, under the paving; its longest horizontal edge is 8.35 cm and total vertical range is 7.35 mm. Another exposed 89.4° face spans 5.66 mm horizontally and 0.15 mm vertically. An exposed example among the largest limit violations spans 16.7 cm horizontally and 2.41 cm vertically but remains extremely thin in projection.

Thus the pre-export LP success should not be restated as exact final-face compliance after native cleanup/export. The JSON preserves coordinates, old/current gradient components, dimensions and paving-relative locations. These small discrepancies did not reproduce the old large black crease in the five reviewed views; their individual close-range visibility has not been established. Do not claim all steep microfaces vanished.
