Read `GOAL.md` and `CLOUD_RESUME.md`. This is a bounded **52f research-branch**
source experiment. It is not integrated into the saved coast56 world, whose
current lineage is 55 → 53west → 51b. No project asset, scene, default, or old

## Actual observation and decision

The author actually inspected `ref/1216.png`, the stabilized52hD A/D world images,
and all five58 source images. The reference requires hard low-poly articulation,
thick irregular lower cloud folds, shallow horizontal upper layers and sparse
peaks, scale progression, and real enclosed valleys rather than black holes to
the water. Increased roundness is not the goal.

The58 first source **fails visual review**. Its continuous lower surface is a
square terrain block with long vertical skirts and a dominant rectangular
underbody. The top reads as peaks on a heightfield. Underside variation is real
but does not change that flat-tray silhouette. Tiny perimeter perturbations and
more noise would not correct the design. The three upper ribbons establish a
more useful horizontal axis, but none is accepted independently.

## Required redesign

The next source must abandon the rectangular top/bottom heightfield construction.
Use editable3D lofted folds with irregular plan shapes, unequal horizontal
sections and independent deep bellies, merging actual overlapping volumes into
a local closed outer shell. Fold endpoints and side curls define the outline;
there is no four-sided trimming boundary or common lower cap. Keep broad
connected low folds under valleys while retaining irregular downward lobes,
overhangs and unequal side returns. Do not merely round a new large block or
repeat equal-size spheres. Preserve this rejected source and its actual images.
