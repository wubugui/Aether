# 58B:3D folded shell redesign, pending actual native construction

The first58 source and all five actual images are frozen by the parent directory's
`revision-a-freeze.json`. SourceA was visually rejected as a rectangular terrain
slab despite closed geometry. Its artifacts and evidence must not be overwritten.

B replaces the method. Eleven authored **closed3D lofts** use unequal lateral
widths, separate upper heights and lower depths, asymmetrical12-point sections,
varying rolls and nonparallel curved axes. The main low saddle connects the
near/far returns beneath the valleys. The eastern return and western side curl
make the outer boundary close by real tapering volumes. There is no rectangular
domain, top/bottom heightfield, cut wall or common underside cap. Two rare sharp
lips sit on broad folds; every ridge must not become a high cone.

Original loft meshes remain native editable control objects. Their temporary
copies are joined and volumetrically remeshed at26m to remove internal faces;
the resulting continuous union is retained as a separate native control mesh.
Native quad-flow remeshing targets4800 faces and restrained flat triangles.
No smooth modifier, sphere, billboard, negative hole-cutting or material change
is used. B preservesA's3 unaccepted upper ribbons exactly to isolate the lower
construction. This reuse is not acceptance of those ribbons.

Fresh readback must check all controls, raw union and final lower mesh for real
closure, connected components and nonadjacent triangle overlaps. Root-local GLB
mapping, original1216 camera point classification, actual seaY0, external21-root
source contacts and sampled outer-boundary distances are recorded again.
Geometric success still gives no visual or full-world pass.

Next source views must reject equal-diameter sausages, repeated round hats, one
large potato-shaped block, a rectangular undersurface, tall straight skirts,
thin neck connections, repetitive high cones or a smooth low-detail shell. The
reference remains hard low-poly with primary/secondary scale differences and
wide saddles. Preserve real failed pixels before another redesign.

Only the parent may schedule the source build or preview window. The prepared
entry `python source-assets/cloud-bank58/revision-b/run_source58b.py` runs only
native build plus a second fresh Blender verification process,2 threads. Input
reports stay under each run's `inputs/`; a new `running/complete:false` result is
written before starting. A unique terminal proof and wrapper exit code identify
completion. No runtime/world route exists in this script, and no58-to56 merge has
been prepared or performed.
