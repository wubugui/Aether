#52g first source prototype: rejected

Five actual900x640 BlenderCPU frames in cloudsea52g-prototype-source-v0-20261001T072103Z-qg5lysu0,process0,all viewed. This is a developer source review, not independent world acceptance.

Front: large upper body becomes a pointed sail. Side/back: regular vertical shell/rib plates appear, particularly three sequential high lobes on the main crown. Top: long narrow nearly parallel trenches dominate instead of broad irregular rounded cloud crests. Underside retains52f disc-like low bodies by design; that known failure is not fixed.

Closed topology and smaller/larger silhouette changes do not rescue the form. This prototype is rejected for world integration and must not expand to three variants. Preserve source and images. RevisionB should focus on one truly thick broad rounded crown with off-center notches and nonparallel transitions, avoiding repetitive axial contraction rings, sharp peaks, color-only changes or simply adding triangles.
